Support

Audiobook Binder for macOS

Questions, bug reports, and support requests go to the developer by email. You do not need an account.

support@audiobook-binder.nwai.cc

Include the macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac), the app version shown next to the title in the window, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps.

What the app does

Audiobook Binder runs on your Mac. It reads a folder of chapter audio you choose and writes one .m4b file with title, author, cover, and chapter marks. It does not upload your library. See the privacy policy for what stays on the computer.

Common questions

Where is the finished file? With “Save in book folder” checked, it is written next to the chapter files. Uncheck that and choose another folder.

Which Macs does it support? macOS 14 or later. The app encodes with the system audio frameworks. It does not need ffmpeg or other installs.

A book was skipped or chapters look wrong. Email the folder layout (names only, not the audio) and the title the app showed. Nested folders are scanned, but a folder with no chapter audio is not treated as a book.

Another way to write

You can also open a public issue: github.com/aitrail001/m4b/issues. Email is enough if you do not use GitHub.

支持

适用于 macOS 的 Audiobook Binder

提问、错误报告和支持请求请发邮件给开发者。不需要注册账号。

support@audiobook-binder.nwai.cc

请写上 macOS 版本（苹果菜单 → 关于本机）、窗口标题旁显示的应用版本，以及出问题时你在做什么。截图会有帮助。

这个应用做什么

Audiobook Binder 在你的 Mac 上运行。它读取你选择的分章音频文件夹，写成一个带书名、作者、封面和章节标记的 .m4b。它不会上传你的书库。哪些信息留在本机，见隐私政策。

常见问题

成品在哪里？ 勾选「保存在书文件夹」时，文件写在分章音频旁边。取消勾选后，请另选一个文件夹。

支持哪些 Mac？ macOS 14 或更高版本。编码使用系统自带的音频框架，不需要 ffmpeg 或其他安装。

一本书被跳过，或章节看起来不对。 请发邮件说明文件夹结构（只要名称，不要发音频）以及应用显示的书名。嵌套文件夹会扫描，但没有分章音频的文件夹不会当成一本书。

其他联系方式

也可以开一个公开 issue：github.com/aitrail001/m4b/issues。不用 GitHub 时，发邮件即可。
